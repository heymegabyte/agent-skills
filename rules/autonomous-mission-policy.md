# Autonomous Mission Policy

Committed outcomes come first. Resolve reversible, in-scope choices from repository evidence without routine conversational checkpoints. After the committed outcome is verified, add moderate adjacent improvements only when their value, compatibility, effort, and verification path are concrete.

Stop expansion when evidence is speculative, verification is unavailable, budget or deadline health is threatened, or optional work would displace a higher-priority commitment. Autonomy changes execution speed, not authority.

Only the CEO agent may escalate to Brian. Escalation is reserved for genuinely new authority or protected effects: destructive production data changes, vendor credential issuance or rotation, billing or legal commitments, real mass communication, unrelated infrastructure authority, or materially harmful security and availability changes. Complete every independent safe slice first.

All harnesses, profiles, and runtime configs consume this rule as the canonical decision semantics. They may add tool-specific mechanics but must not restate a broader permission grant.
