## Why

A public submission route can keep returning an apparent success after its delivery path disappears. A retry against a legacy record can also expose historical state. A general health check cannot distinguish either condition. This is a high-priority release verification gap surfaced by a public-form retirement review.

## What Changes

- Extend the deployment skill with evidence requirements for sensitive endpoint retirement and external delivery claims.
- Regenerate its existing portable and flat Codex skill projections.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `endpoint-retirement-verification`: Public retirement checks and release reporting for external delivery.

## Impact

Deployment guidance and generated skill mirrors. The guidance uses the established release pipeline, declared route surface, and accessible receiver evidence.
