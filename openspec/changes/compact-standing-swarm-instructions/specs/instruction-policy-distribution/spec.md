## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Standing swarm instructions retain applicable requirements within budget

The standing swarm rule SHALL retain its execution stages and authority constraints within the configured instruction budget. Its full planning catalog SHALL remain available on demand.

#### Scenario: An agent needs a swarm planning strategy

- **WHEN** an agent follows the standing rule's catalog link
- **THEN** the full planning catalog is available through a declared skill reference in the packaged assets
