# K — IDENTITY, BACKEND GUARANTEES, PRIVACY, AND EDITORIAL GOVERNANCE

Load this category only when the article task, affected runtime boundary, or release risk selects it. The directives below are exact authorized requirements. Record applicability and live evidence in the adopting repository; policy presence is not runtime verification.

- **K01.** Configure authentication separately for each controlled website using real Google and GitHub OAuth applications and a working magic-link email service. Validate deployment origins and callback URLs; a reusable integration does not confer editing access to arbitrary websites.
- **K02.** Enforce authentication and authorization on every read of private drafts, write, upload, publication, history operation, and collaboration connection. Hiding controls or setting an editor readOnly is presentation behavior and must never substitute for a server permission check.
- **K03.** Derive tenant and website context from the verified session and authorized resource relationships. Apply it to database queries, assets, exports, job payloads, and room membership; reject client-supplied cross-tenant identifiers even when they reference valid resources.
- **K04.** Define explicit capabilities for owners, editors, authors, reviewers, and viewers, adapting names to the existing application. Separate drafting, reviewing, publishing, inviting, deleting, and configuring providers so a role gains only the actions its workflow requires.
- **K05.** Link Google, GitHub, and email identities only through the authentication framework’s verified, trusted-account rules and an authenticated linking flow. Handle conflicts explicitly; matching email strings alone must never merge accounts or transfer existing editorial privileges. [S49]
- **K06.** Use the authentication framework’s supported OAuth flow and its state, redirect, and PKCE protections where applicable. Allowlist redirect destinations, reject open redirects, and test cancellation, expired callbacks, provider errors, and an already-linked account.
- **K07.** Make magic-link tokens expiring and single-use, store them hashed where supported, and redact tokens from logs. Rate-limit requests and verification attempts, avoid account-enumeration responses, and preserve a safe destination after successful verification. [S21]
- **K08.** Treat email delivery as a real operational dependency with verified sending configuration, delivery failures, retries, and observable status. Keep invitation and login messages transactional, and do not claim magic-link authentication works until the complete delivery-and-verification flow succeeds.
- **K09.** Use secure session cookies and the framework’s CSRF and origin protections; configure lifetimes and renewal deliberately. Revoke sessions when required, respect logout, and ensure server-side mutations cannot be triggered by an untrusted cross-origin page.
- **K10.** Apply per-account, per-tenant, and appropriate network rate limits to login, invitations, new drafts, uploads, AI requests, and publication endpoints. Use bounded work queues and clear retry behavior so one noisy user cannot exhaust shared capacity.
- **K11.** Make editorial membership invitation-based or explicitly provisioned according to site policy. Signing in or self-registering grants no authoring privilege by itself; invitations expire, bind to the intended identity, and cannot silently escalate roles.
- **K12.** Give scheduled publishing workers narrowly scoped service identities with explicit website and action permissions. Keep DataForSEO, image, email, storage, and authentication secrets server-side, redact them from telemetry, and support rotation without exposing credentials in article documents.
- **K13.** Maintain an append-only audit trail for membership changes, draft writes, reviews, restores, scheduling, publishing, and unpublishing. Record actor, tenant, document, revision, operation, and time while excluding tokens, raw credentials, and unnecessary private research content.
- **K14.** Validate every incoming document, component property, query, and job payload against explicit server-side schemas and size limits. Reject unsupported node types, malformed identifiers, excessive nesting, and unexpected executable fields before persistence or rendering.
- **K15.** Validate uploads using actual file signatures, permitted media types, dimensions, and byte limits rather than trusting filenames or browser headers. Reject or safely sanitize active formats, generate safe derivatives, and enforce tenant ownership before attaching an asset.
- **K16.** Route source and asset fetching through a guarded server service that validates destinations and every redirect. Block loopback, private networks, credential-bearing URLs, unsupported schemes, oversized responses, and decompression abuse; recheck resolved addresses to reduce SSRF exposure.
- **K17.** Render only registered components and sanitized rich-text content. Reject executable HTML, script URLs, unsafe embeds, and arbitrary MDX evaluation at both ingestion and rendering boundaries; apply the application’s content security policy to approved external resources.
- **K18.** Make write endpoints idempotent and protect ordinary draft saves with expected revisions or equivalent compare-and-swap checks. For collaborative documents, persist CRDT updates through the room authority rather than allowing stale whole-document autosaves to overwrite shared changes.
- **K19.** Isolate private draft caches and recovery data by user, tenant, and document, with deliberate retention and logout cleanup. Minimize stored personal information and make deletion and export flows cover local recovery, server drafts, and derived artifacts.
- **K20.** Authorize collaboration handshakes against current document membership, validate room identifiers, and recheck long-lived access after revocation. Disconnect revoked participants and prevent public presence metadata from revealing private article titles, emails, or another tenant’s activity.
- **K21.** Acknowledge collaborative writes only after the required durable persistence succeeds, and reconstruct rooms from persisted updates and snapshots after restart or hibernation. Test recovery explicitly; an in-memory Yjs document is not a durable storage strategy. [S51]
- **K22.** Publish an immutable, validated snapshot using an atomic state transition that binds content, metadata, references, and approved assets. Reject stale approvals or missing dependencies, and ensure a failed publish cannot expose a partially assembled article.
- **K23.** Enforce the three-daily-publication policy with timezone-aware slots, unique execution keys, leases, and recorded outcomes. Retries, overlapping workers, restarts, and daylight-saving changes must not produce duplicate posts or silently increase the authorized publication count.
- **K24.** Block publication when externally checkable claims lack verified supporting sources or citations misrepresent what a source establishes. Preserve access timestamps and relevant excerpts, distinguish reported facts from analysis, and never fabricate quotations, credentials, statistics, or firsthand experience.
- **K25.** Validate every numerical figure against its stored dataset, units, timeframe, transformation, and cited source. Check calculations and caption consistency; an AI-generated raster cannot serve as evidence for precise values, axes, labels, or quantitative comparisons.
- **K26.** Require an editorial release gate for originality, selected-topic relevance, brand fit, clear structure, factual support, and the requested substantial article length. Fail incomplete or padded drafts, and preserve actionable failure reasons for revision instead of publishing to satisfy cadence.
- **K27.** Define content-specific escalation rules for unsupported high-stakes advice, legal accusations, sensitive personal data, and disputed claims. Apply only the rules relevant to the article, use a qualified review path when needed, and preserve the reviewer’s recorded decision.
- **K28.** Track provenance and permitted use for supplied assets, generated imagery, quoted material, datasets, and imported articles. Preserve attribution requirements, separate illustrative content from evidence, and honor removal requests through dependent articles and derivative assets.
- **K29.** Enforce research and image budgets with atomic reservations, provider cost records, retry ceilings, and reconciliation. Concurrent jobs cannot each spend the full remaining allowance; a missing price or uncertain charge must produce a visible budget exception.
- **K30.** Provide tested rollback, backup, retention, deletion, and export procedures for articles, source ledgers, assets, memberships, and job state. Verify restored permissions and publication pointers, and document how an operator can halt scheduling during a publishing incident.
