# J — MEASUREMENT, DISTRIBUTION, CONVERSION, AND MAINTENANCE

Load this category only when the article task, affected runtime boundary, or release risk selects it. The directives below are exact authorized requirements. Record applicability and live evidence in the adopting repository; policy presence is not runtime verification.

- **J01.** Define success per article before publication: a reader outcome, a relevant search intent, and a meaningful site action, rather than word count or raw output volume.
- **J02.** Connect authorized Search Console properties and retain page/query/device/country/date dimensions needed for decision-making; record data freshness and missing-data limitations. [S08]
- **J03.** Use DataForSEO as a consistent external search dataset and Search Console as first-party search performance; do not pretend their metrics are interchangeable.
- **J04.** Measure qualified visits, engaged reading, useful component interactions, and conversions through the existing PostHog/GA stack without duplicate event firing.
- **J05.** Use one versioned event contract across sites and component packages, with article/revision/component identifiers and appropriate consent controls.
- **J06.** Track citation usage to learn whether evidence is accessible, but do not optimize citation clicks as if more clicks necessarily mean greater trust or accuracy.
- **J07.** Measure calculator, comparison, copy-code, download, and chart-table interactions by reader benefit; avoid invasive recording of private editor content or input values.
- **J08.** Segment article results by intent, topic cluster, format, site maturity, device, country, and acquisition source so aggregate averages do not hide useful patterns.
- **J09.** Detect high-impression low-CTR opportunities and propose truthful title/deck improvements; consider search position and SERP layout before attributing the problem to wording.
- **J10.** Detect pages that attract the wrong intent and revise positioning or internal links; do not simply add more exact-match phrases.
- **J11.** Monitor topic cannibalization and query overlap between articles over time; recommend consolidation, clear differentiation, or deliberate navigation before mass rewrites.
- **J12.** Build a decaying-content queue from factual age, broken sources, product releases, traffic changes, and reader feedback; score urgency by actual impact.
- **J13.** Schedule source-sensitive refreshes for prices, availability, instructions, and benchmarks, while keeping stable explanatory articles on a lighter review cadence.
- **J14.** Re-run affected calculations, comparisons, screenshots, and charts when an underlying fact changes; a text-only update must not leave old evidence in the visuals.
- **J15.** Publish corrections transparently and retain the previous revision. Distinguish corrected errors from ordinary expansion or visual refinement.
- **J16.** Add a useful next action matched to the article's intent, such as a relevant tool, example project, comparison, template, or product feature.
- **J17.** Keep conversion elements proportionate and contextual; do not interrupt comprehension with repeated banners, obstructive pop-ups, or misleading buttons.
- **J18.** Offer related reading based on the next question a reader is likely to have; prevent every article from linking to the same arbitrary promotional destinations.
- **J19.** Maintain topical hub pages that summarize the subject and guide readers through prerequisites, deep dives, comparisons, and practical implementations.
- **J20.** Generate accurate social excerpts and preview assets from the approved revision; queue distribution only through channels and accounts already authorized for publishing.
- **J21.** Produce optional newsletter summaries or RSS excerpts that preserve caveats and link to the source article; never automatically email people because a blog was published.
- **J22.** Create downloadable assets when the article earns them, such as a cited dataset, checklist, or configuration template, with version and license metadata.
- **J23.** Repurpose suitable articles into captioned video, TTS, or presentation formats using approved scripts and existing tooling; keep the article as the canonical factual record.
- **J24.** Localize by researching local intent, examples, units, and terminology; translation alone must not be treated as sufficient new research for a different market.
- **J25.** Experiment with layouts and component choices through existing feature infrastructure; preserve accessibility and avoid treating traffic fluctuations as proof of causation.
- **J26.** Compare matched periods and reasonable cohorts, track uncertainty, and wait for sufficient observations before making SEO or conversion claims from small samples.
- **J27.** Evaluate image providers and article recipes using acceptance rate, revision effort, page performance, and reader value rather than the cheapest billed generation alone.
- **J28.** Feed verified editorial findings back into ranking criteria, component recipes, and agent skills through reviewed changes; do not automatically promote untested correlations to rules.
- **J29.** Archive, merge, or redirect obsolete content only with a clear editorial and URL-preservation decision; avoid bulk deletion triggered solely by low traffic.
- **J30.** Produce a concise weekly operations dashboard showing published versus planned articles, missed slots, spend, evidence issues, useful outcomes, and the highest-value next improvements.
